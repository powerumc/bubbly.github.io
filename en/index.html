FAQ

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon Macs with macOS 15 or later. Intel Macs aren't supported.

Is it on the App Store?

No. Download the Apple-notarized DMG from this site. You can check for and install new versions in the app.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Bubbly from the menu bar and move it from Applications to the Trash. To remove your notification history too, clear it first in Settings › History.
