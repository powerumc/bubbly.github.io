Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rivon Labs ("we" or "us") handles personal information in operating Bubbly (the "Software") and this website. It covers what Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and Article 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) require us to tell you. This policy is provided in Korean and English; if the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

In short: the content of your notifications never leaves your Mac. The Software has no ads and collects no usage analytics. This website uses Google Analytics for visitor statistics (see 4 below). Apart from that, the only personal information we receive is Pro purchase and license information, and the emails you send us.

1. Information that stays on your Mac

The following is processed only on your Mac and is never sent to us. We cannot receive or see it.

  • Notification content (app name, title, body, icon): used to redraw notifications on your screen.
  • History: past notifications are saved in a file on your Mac (on by default, kept for 30 days). Only your user account can read the file (permission 0600). You can change how long to keep it (7, 30, or 90 days), turn history off, clear it, or leave out specific apps.
  • Settings: per-app rules (the names and bundle IDs of apps you choose), shortcuts, and so on.
  • License key: when you activate Pro, it is stored in your Mac's keychain.
  • Logs: troubleshooting logs record only the length of notification text, not the text itself.
  • Diagnostic files: "Report a Problem" creates a file that you save yourself. The Software never sends it anywhere. We receive it only if you attach it to an email (see 2-3).

4. This website

  • Visitor statistics (Google Analytics): To improve the website, we use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to see visitor statistics. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about how you use the website, such as the pages you visit, how you arrived, your device and browser, and your approximate location estimated from your IP address. It does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address, and we do not combine this information with other information or use it for advertising. The information is processed on Google's servers, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States, and individual usage records are deleted after two months. The cookies stay in your browser for up to two years after your last visit. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • How to opt out: Block cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, and nothing is collected. Nothing is sent if you turn on Do Not Track in your browser either. Opting out does not limit your use of the website.
  • Visitors in the EU, EEA, and UK: The legal basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in improving the website), and you can object at any time using the methods above (Article 21). Safeguards for international transfers are in 6 below.
  • Apart from Google Analytics, there are no tracking tools or scripts or fonts loaded from elsewhere.
  • To remember your language choice (Korean or English) for your next visit, we store it only in your browser's local storage. It is not sent to us, and it is removed when you clear the site's data in your browser.
  • This website is served by GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process access records (IP address, time, browser information); we do not receive them.
  • Cloudflare resolves our domain name (DNS). Your connection goes directly to GitHub and does not pass through Cloudflare.

7. Deletion

When a retention period ends or the purpose is achieved, we delete the information without delay. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered (for email, including the trash), and information held by Polar is deactivated or deleted at our request to Polar. Information we must keep by law is kept separately, and only for the required period.

8. Your rights

  • You may ask us to give you access to, correct, delete, or stop processing your personal information (PIPA Arts. 35–37). If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, you also have the GDPR rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21).
  • Email contact@rivonlabs.com. A representative (a legal guardian or a person you authorize) may also make the request. We will reply within 10 days after confirming your identity (within one month where the GDPR applies).
  • We do not hold the information on your Mac (section 1), so you delete it yourself: clear history in Settings › History, and deleting the Software lets you remove the rest. You can deactivate Macs in Polar's customer portal, and you can also deactivate this Mac from the Pro tab in the Software.
  • You may file a complaint with the Personal Information Protection Commission of Korea. If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country (GDPR Art. 77).

9. Security measures

  • The Software: notification content never leaves your Mac; history files and folders can be opened only by your user account, and the text of deleted records is overwritten in the file. The license key is kept in the keychain. Updates and compatibility settings are used only after their signatures are verified. Internet connections use HTTPS.
  • Rivon Labs: we run no servers that hold personal information. Our Polar and email accounts are used only by the person in charge and are protected with two-step verification.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. A child under 14 needs the consent of a legal guardian to buy Pro.
